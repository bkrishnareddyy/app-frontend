  • Item identity (name, SKU, brand, model) is separated from the classification decision made against it
  • Classification is proposed and approved per jurisdiction, against evidence — not typed in blind
  • Identifiers (SKU, GTIN, UPC, supplier part number) let incoming shipment lines auto-match to the right product
  • Filters surface unreviewed items, unclassified items, and anything awaiting revalidation
How to on Qubere
Sidebar → Trade Data → Products → Add product.

Party Master

One verified record for every importer, supplier, and broker you work with.

A party holds identity only — name, identifiers, registrations, and roles — each with its own status and evidence. Screening runs against this record; it doesn't invent one.

Use Case The same supplier shows up under slightly different names across old shipment records, and restricted-party screening needs one clean identity to check against.
  • Legal name, identifiers (EORI, D-U-N-S, VAT, Tax ID), and registrations tracked as separate, evidenced facts
  • Roles (Importer, Exporter, Manufacturer, Customs Broker, etc.) assigned per relationship
  • Review status (Unreviewed, Needs Review, Awaiting Revalidation) flags stale records automatically
  • Client-scoped or account-wide — the same party record works across every shipment it touches
How to on Qubere
Sidebar → Trade Data → Parties → Add party.
