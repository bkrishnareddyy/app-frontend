## Cross-cutting Quality Standards violations found

1. **No fake data, ever (Standard #1)** — repeatedly violated:
   - `opportunities/scan/route.ts:60,105` — hardcoded 7.5%/2.8% "estimated" refund percentages.
   - `drawback.service.ts:47-49` and `landedCost.ts:56-58` — hardcoded `"2.8%"` general rate / `"List3"` Section 301 tranche used as a stand-in for real HTS lookup, in two separate places, even though a real lookup path (`loadHtsCodesMap`/`HtsNodeRepository`) already exists and is used correctly elsewhere in the same codebase.
   - `simulator/page.tsx:137-138,146` — hardcoded client-side duty rates and an arbitrary breakeven formula.
   - `regulatory-ingest/route.ts:46-57` — hardcoded mock Federal Register document served on fetch failure.
   - `regulatory-ingest/route.ts:117` — hardcoded fallback `affectedHtsCodes: ["9903.88.67"]` when no AI key is configured.
   - `prisma/schema.prisma:1122` — `DrawbackClaim.cbpClaimNumber @default("DBK-2026-9901")`, a fake-looking default.
   - `src/scripts/seed-drawback.ts` — the only real source of `DrawbackLot` data in the running app, because B-2's real pipeline is dead code.

2. **Money is always Decimal.js (Standard #2)** — violated in `psc/route.ts:15-16,56-62` (plain `number`/`Math.max`) and `simulator/page.tsx:137-146` (plain float arithmetic client-side).

3. **Tenant isolation (Standard #3)** — critically violated in `regulatory-ingest/route.ts:145`: `db.accountMembership.findMany()` with **no `where` clause**, fanning notifications out to every membership in the database regardless of account, on every ingest run. This is the most severe standards violation found in either feature.

4. **One Vitest test per capability (Standard #4)** — several capabilities have zero coverage: F09-A (opportunity detection), F09-E (reconciliation), F10-A (regulatory ingest itself, as opposed to impact analysis), F10-C (scenario modeling / USMCA). Where tests do exist for the affected areas (`tests/unit/regulatory.test.ts:68-69`), they encode the fake `0.017` rate delta as the expected correct answer, effectively locking in the bug.

5. **`any` types (Standard #7)** — `src/app/api/regulatory/[id]/impact-analysis/route.ts:24`, `.../impacted/route.ts:29` (`update.metadata as any`), `regulatory-ingest/route.ts:74` (`let extracted: any`).

6. **Pagination (Standard #8)** — `GET /api/simulator/scenarios` (`scenarios/route.ts:6-17`) and `GET /api/refunds/psc` (`psc/route.ts:19-31`) are unbounded `findMany` with no cursor/limit.

7. **Idempotency-Key (Standard #9)** — present on `drawback/match` and `drawback/claims` (`checkIdempotency`/`persistIdempotency` calls) but absent from `refunds/opportunities/scan/route.ts` and `refunds/psc/route.ts`, both real mutating POSTs.
